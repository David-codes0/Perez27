Our Love Story

Every love story is beautiful, but ours is our favorite

The Beginning

It all began with a seemingly harmless “Hi” on Facebook in 2020 without expectations, without ulterior motives, and certainly without knowing where that simple greeting would lead us.

One pleasant conversation led to another. We exchanged contacts, conversations became friendships, and somewhere along the way, we exchanged something far more precious — our hearts.

What began so simply has brought us to this beautiful place we now call love.

Beyond the Feelings

We soon discovered that love is more than butterflies in the belly or the excitement that comes with being together. Feelings may start a relationship, but they cannot sustain it.

For us, love has become a choice, a commitment to stand by each other through thick and thin.

We have laughed together, prayed together, grown together, and walked through difficult moments together. There have been rough seasons, but rather than pulling us apart, they have strengthened the bond we share and taught us that true love is not merely about how we feel about each other, but about the decision to keep choosing each other.

Becoming One

And now, as it pleases the Lord, two hearts are becoming one.

As we prepare to walk down the aisle and make a lifelong covenant before God, we are filled with gratitude and wonder at how far He has brought us.

From a simple “Hi” in 2020 to standing at the threshold of forever, our story is a beautiful reminder that sometimes, God can hide something extraordinary in the simplest of beginnings.

We are excited for the journey ahead, trusting God to fulfil His promises concerning our union and to make our home a testimony of His love, grace, and faithfulness.

This is our story.
This is our love.
And by God’s grace, this is only the beginning.
